# R77 — QA checklist (APK 0.1.442)

## Our places (Remember › Our places)
1. Been there: add "Our café" (Café, city, rating, first visit date) → card shows city, 1 visit, stars.
2. Open it → "We were here again" adds a dated visit with a caption; "Add with photos" opens a memory pre-filled with the place; after saving, the visit opens the photos.
3. Long-press a visit → remove (the memory stays).
4. Bucket list: add 2 places in Paris → tick both → "Plan a trip (2)" → trip editor titled "Trip to Paris" with the list in notes → save → both show "In a planned trip".
5. "We went!" on a bucket-list place moves it to Been there.
6. ⋮ › Open in Maps; star = favourite; filter chips by kind and favourites.
7. Partner gets "… added a place"; a private place is invisible to the partner.

## Counters (Remember › Counters)
8. Add "First date" 2 Mar 2020 → card shows "6 years / 8 months / since"; detail shows months, weeks, days, the next milestone (e.g. 2,500 days) and its date.
9. Add a future "Trip" → "3 weeks 2 days / to go" style; under 14 days shows days; tomorrow shows "Tomorrow".
10. "Every year" (wedding) counts down to the next anniversary and shows "Since the first one".
11. Link "Wedding" to "First date" → detail shows "1 year 1 month after First date"; "Pick" compares with any other counter.
12. Pin → shown big at the top. Timeline icon → future above the Today line, past below.
13. Anniversary/birthdays from Settings, last met/next meet (apart) and the next trip appear by themselves.
14. Add photos and the story → memory linked; Arabic: "سنتان و3 أشهر", "15 يومًا".
