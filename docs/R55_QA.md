# R55 — Consistency pass: two-phone test script

Install 0.1.4xx as an in-place update on both phones. Phone A = Ahmed, Phone B = Kandice.

| # | Where | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any list screen | Tap the filter icon | Panel drops down under the icon: ✕ at top, Status Active/Removed as chips, Clear + Apply pinned at the bottom. Nothing changes until Apply. A "Showing: …" line appears under the title; tap Clear there to reset. No refresh buttons anywhere (pull down instead). |
| 2 | Today | Look lower right | "Quick add" floating button. Shortcuts in two groups: Plan together / Stay close. |
| 3 | Calendar, To-do lists, Reminders, Notes, Occasions, Contact cards, Activities, Trips, Shared experiences | Look lower right | One floating "Add …" / "New list" / "Start activity" button with a plain +. No add buttons at the top. Calendar has no Birthday/Anniversary/Special Day buttons and no Coming up. |
| 4 | Any editor (new or edit) | Open it | Full page, Cancel + Save at the bottom. Save disabled until something changes and every * field is filled. Back with changes asks to discard. |
| 5 | To-do lists | New list → name + items (one per line) → Save | List opens with those items. Card shows "N of M done", no Related notes. Search "Search lists" works. ⋮ order: Mark complete, Cancel list, Reopen, Edit, Change log. |
| 6 | Reminders | Look at a card | Coloured time block (Today/Tomorrow + time). ✓ for Done, ⋮ with Done / Follow-up / Snooze, Edit, Remove, Change log. |
| 7 | Notes, Memories, Trips, Occasions | ⋮ on an item | Edit/Open, Remove (asks first) / Restore, Change log. Removed item reappears under Status = Removed with Restore. |
| 8 | Contact cards | Look | Title "Contact cards"; "Add contact card" lower right; small Import-from-phone button lower left; no extra clock/gear icon. |
| 9 | A little love | Look | No "Remind me…" button. "Create my own gesture" opens a page. Send button says "Send to your <label>". |
| 10 | Today (Phone A, no love sent for 1+ day while apart, 09:00–21:00) | Look at Needs attention | "Send a little love" nudge; "Not today" hides it until tomorrow. |
| 11 | Daily connection | Open | Two tabs. Daily check-in tab FAB "Check on Kay" opens a page with Cancel/Send. Feelings journal FAB "Record my feeling". Cards show who · mood in words/colour, the message; "Waiting for your answer" badge when it is yours to answer. |
| 12 | Shared experiences | Open (first time in 30 min) | "What shall we do together?" pop-up; ✕ or tap outside closes it; reopening within 30 min shows nothing. Tap a collection tile → its own page. Searching on the main page shows results. |
| 13 | Memories | Tap the FAB | Fold-up: Story journal, Daily selfie, Scrapbook studio. Daily selfie → Selfie studio opens the front camera, polaroid preview, caption, Shared/Only me, week strip; Save selfie disabled until there is a photo. |
| 14 | Letters | Open, wait 2 min | No "Unable to load letters". "Received" fits on one line. Cards show date and time. |
| 15 | Phone gallery / Files | Share a photo to OneSpace | "Save shared photo" page: Save to Items to sort or Memories, Only me / Both of us. In Items to sort the photo chip opens it. |
| 16 | Files / Gmail | Share a PDF to OneSpace | "Save shared PDF" → Items to sort with the PDF chip. Phone B cannot open it until it is shared with them. A file over 10 MB shows a clear message. |
| 17 | Activities | Start activity → Plan an event → Save | Activity opens with 4 open steps already added. "Just a note" adds none. |
| 18 | World | Look | Subtitle only about trips; no "People & contacts live in Remember"; no refresh button. |
