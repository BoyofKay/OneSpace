## Not done yet (honest list)

- Share-in still accepts **links and text** only (plus contact cards). Photos and PDFs from other apps need Android work and are on the later list.
- The per-item **⋮ menu** (final status first, then Edit, Remove, Change log) and the **status look** in lists (strikethrough/green check/red cross/amber Missed) are not applied to every screen yet; history and Remove wording are.
- The "Showing: … ✕" line exists as a component but is only wired on Items to sort.
- To-do creation still uses its bottom sheet (no dialog); events, notes, reminders, trips, memories already used full pages.
- Activity purpose starters fill an outline in the details; they do not yet pre-add option/checklist sections.
- Activities have their own older audit trail, so the Activities screen has no clock icon yet.
- Seasonal theme preview stays on Today; "On this day" memories are not added yet.

## Test script (two phones on this build)

1. **Today opens instantly:** close and reopen the app. Today appears straight away from the saved copy, then refreshes. Turn on flight mode and reopen: you still see Today with "Last updated … · Retry".
2. **Greeting:** "Good morning/afternoon/evening, <name>" and "Your space with your …".
3. **How we live:** tap the house/plane icon on the hero, choose the other option. It saves; **Undo** appears for 10 seconds; your partner gets a push. Switching Apart → Together asks to save "Our journey apart"; check Memories. Switch back with Undo.
4. **Good time to talk:** (only shown when your time zones differ) set work and sleep hours in Profile for both of you; the hero shows the next shared free window.
5. **Since you were away:** on K, send A a hug and add a to-do. On A, open Today: both appear under "Since you were away"; tap one, it opens. Reopen Today: the section folds to one line.
6. **Shortcuts:** every feature has a tile; Letters/Reminders/Items to sort show a count only when something waits.
7. **Quick add:** tap "+ Quick add" → To-do opens the to-do editor; Something to research opens the new Activity page.
8. **New Activity:** in Coordinate tap +, pick "Buy something" (outline appears), type a title, Create. Then tap + again and go back without a title: nothing is created.
9. **Filter panel:** in Notes, tap the slider icon: the panel drops down under it; tick **Removed**, Apply: removed notes show; Clear resets.
10. **Change log:** in Notes tap the clock: see who did what; tap "Me", then "Removed"; Restore a removed note from its row.
11. **Wording:** item menus say Remove/Restore, not Archive; editors say "Add new …"/"Edit …".
12. **Share-in:** from Chrome share an Amazon product to OneSpace: "Save to Items to sort" first; suggestions include Gift idea and Option in an Activity. Tap Save to Items to sort → the tray opens. On Today the tile and "1 item to sort" appear. Tap Sort → Gift idea → choose an occasion → it lands in that occasion's plan and leaves the tray.
13. **Privacy:** the tray item is invisible on the partner's phone until you choose "Share with my partner" in its ⋮ menu.
