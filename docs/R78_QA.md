# R78 — QA checklist (APK 0.1.443)

## Share from Google Maps
1. Google Maps › a place › Share › OneSpace → the first suggestions are "Bucket list place" and "Place we have been".
2. Choose one → the place editor opens with the name, address and link; the card says "On our map" (pin read from the link). Save.
3. A place typed by hand (name + city + country) → Save or "Find" → on the map. Unknown name → message to paste a link.

## Map
4. Our places › map icon → hearts (been there) and compasses (bucket list); filter chips; tap a pin → card → open the place.
5. A place page shows a small map; tapping it opens Google Maps (the shared link when there is one).

## Counters
6. Anniversary set in Settings with the year → two cards: "The day we became us" (the day itself, years ago) and "Our anniversary" (6th anniversary, first on …, coming up).
7. A counter with "Every year" shows the same pair; its page shows "The day itself" and "6th anniversary" side by side.
8. Coming-up cards have a progress bar with %; the page says "N% of the wait is behind us".
9. All: a "Today" line between coming up and looking back; past cards have a soft bar — longer = further back.
10. Link two counters (or Pick) → a line with both dates, today and the time between them.

## Today
11. Our space › Stay close shows Our places and Counters.
