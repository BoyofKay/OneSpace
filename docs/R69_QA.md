# R69 QA — Settings (currency, special dates, holidays) and Memories

## Settings
1. Me › Language and currency: pick SAR / USD / EGP… → Save. New trip flight option, occasion budget, gift, trip item and share-in price start with that currency.
2. Settings › Birthdays, anniversary & special dates: Your birthday, Partner's birthday, Our anniversary, Our own special dates (+ Add). Pick a date → "Keep the year?" → the row shows the date and "next: turns 41" / "8 years". ⋮ › Change / Remove.
3. Calendar shows them on their day every year (tap → this settings page).
4. New occasion › Pick a special date or holiday: the saved dates are first, then yearly calendar events, then holidays. No Kind chips.
5. Settings › Regional holidays: a new space has your countries ticked automatically (from each person's time zone). Saudi Arabia shows Founding Day, National Day, Ramadan begins, Day of Arafah, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha (expected). Egypt and UAE likewise; US/UK/Canada come from Nager.Date.

## Memories
6. Tap a story card → it opens to read; Edit (pencil) to change; share icon → Save or share as PDF / Send by email.
7. Story with layout "Stack (swipe)": swipe the photo deck left/right; Slideshow swipes full photos. Try Mosaic, Film strip, Scattered, Timeline.
8. New story: type a title → Add photos → the photo area appears without leaving.
9. Daily selfie: Ahmed posts a shared selfie; Kandice opens Daily selfie → "Add yours to today's selfie" → both photos side by side in one memory. PDF includes both.
10. Scrapbook: + › Scrapbook studio → choose Both of us / Only me → tap a design (22, grouped) → it opens at once; tap the title on the page to name it; tap photo spots and texts; page chips show • for unsaved pages; Save. Back with unsaved changes → asked to save.
11. New page: blank, the first page keeps its photos. ⋮ › Remove page (not on the last page). ⋮ › Change design keeps words and photos.
12. Tap a scrapbook card → reader: swipe pages, 1 / N, Edit, share → PDF of all pages or email.

All of the above in Arabic (PDF text in Arabic renders right-to-left).
