GETTING STARTED

Your first steps together

A simple introduction to your shared space. Some options may vary during the private test.

1. Create your account

Install the test app using the invitation from the OneSpace team. Create an account and follow the welcome steps.

2. Invite your partner

Create your shared space and send your partner the invitation from the welcome screen. Once accepted, shared items appear for both of you.

3. Set your preferences

Choose your language, theme, work and sleep hours, and notification settings. Each partner can personalize their own experience.

4. Add your first shared plan

Start with a calendar event, to-do or activity. Mark it Shared so your partner can see it, or Only me to keep it private.

5. Stay connected

Try a daily check-in, a letter or a small gesture. Use Today to find what needs attention.

Need a hand?

Visit support for help with sign-in, reminders, account settings or technical issues.
