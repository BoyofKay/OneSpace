Made for real couples' lives

OneSpace adapts to how you live — and stays out of the way.

  • Apart or together. Both clocks and weathers on Today, a countdown to when you meet — or none of that when you share a home.
  • Arabic and English. Each of you chooses your language; the whole app, notifications included, follows.
  • Respects your sleep. Nothing arrives during your night; it waits for a gentle morning summary.
  • Share from any app. A product, a place on Google Maps, a flight or a film goes straight into the right list.

Questions

Does my partner need the app too?

Yes — OneSpace connects exactly two people. One of you creates the space and invites the other with a link or a QR code.

How much does it cost?

It is free during the test.

Is there an iPhone version?

Coming soon. The test starts on Android; iPhone testers will join through Apple's TestFlight.

Can I use it in Arabic?

Yes. Each partner picks Arabic or English, and the layout follows (right to left in Arabic).

What happens to our things if one of us leaves?

Shared items stay with the other person; private items are deleted. See Delete your account.
